Mission Control AI is presented in the AAFC Builders portfolio as a tool for organizing nonprofit operations.

## The problem it takes on

Program notes, participant updates, and follow-up tasks can end up in different places. Teams need a way to see what needs attention without losing the context behind a number.

## The proposed approach

The public project description covers dashboards, briefs, follow-up tracking, human review, and evaluation tests. Those are design goals and documented capabilities to inspect, not proof of a live customer deployment.

## Where to inspect it

Open the [public repository](https://github.com/knoxkiminou1-byte/mission-control-ai). Read the README, product brief, and test instructions. Check the code and the latest changes before relying on a feature.

## What to look for

A useful test is whether the system separates facts from AI-generated summaries and keeps a person in control of consequential decisions.
