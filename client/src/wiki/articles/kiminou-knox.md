Kiminou Knox is an author, poet, and director at Artists and Athletes for Change. He is from East Palo Alto, was raised in the Bay Area, and is based in New Orleans.

## Writing and building

Writing teaches you to pay attention to what somebody actually hears. A website has a similar job. Somebody lands on a page, reads a few lines, and decides whether to stay.

The words have to make sense. The design has to help them move. When either one gets in the way, the work needs another pass.

## AAFC Builders

Kiminou directs work at AAFC, including the Builders website and its public project showcase. The site gives visitors a place to see the work, learn about the tools, and understand how a project gets made.
