AAFC stands for Artists and Athletes for Change. The name says who we want at the table and what kind of work we want to do.

## The starting point

People have ideas, skills, and audiences. A lot of them still run into the same problem: they need a working website, a better way to organize information, or a place to show what they have made.

AAFC Builders works on that practical side. We make digital tools and sites that people can actually use.

## Who this work is for

Artists need places to show their work. Athletes need a way to present their story beyond a stat line. Community organizations need tools that make their work easier to run. Small businesses need customers to find them and understand what they offer.

Those needs are different, so the builds should be different too.

## How to judge the work

Open the site on your phone. Try the links. Find the contact information. See whether you understand the offer without somebody standing beside you explaining it.

A project should be able to answer those simple tests. The rest is what we improve from there.
