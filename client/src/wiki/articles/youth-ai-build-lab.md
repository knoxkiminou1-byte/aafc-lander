Youth AI Build Lab is a project for young people turning ideas into something they can show and improve.

## The idea

A student might start with a business concept, a community project, or a creative portfolio. The tool is intended to help them work through the steps and produce useful materials.

## The project design

The AAFC Builders showcase describes guided coaching, feedback rubrics, mentor review, and exportable project pages. Check the repository to see which parts are implemented and tested.

## Why human review belongs here

Feedback from software can help somebody revise a draft. A mentor can ask whether the idea fits the person and the people it is supposed to serve.
