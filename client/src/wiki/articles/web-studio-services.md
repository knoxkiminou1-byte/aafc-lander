A website has a job. Sometimes that job is getting bookings. Sometimes it is showing a portfolio. Sometimes it is helping a team keep track of its own work.

## Websites and landing pages

We can plan and build pages for artists, performers, small businesses, and community projects. That includes clear navigation, mobile layouts, contact paths, and space for the work itself.

## Project showcases

A portfolio should show what somebody made and let people open it. We use project pages, screenshots, direct links, and plain descriptions when those materials are available.

## Interactive tools

Some ideas need more than a page. Forms, dashboards, search tools, and small applications can make sense when they solve a specific problem.

## Before starting

Tell us who will use the project, what they need to do, and what you already have. A useful first conversation can save days of building the wrong thing.
