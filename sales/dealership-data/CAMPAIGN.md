# Dealership Data Campaign — Draft-Only Launch Package

Owner: AAFC / SALES & GROWTH

Authoritative workflow from Rashida:

Lead List → Email #1 → if reply YES → send data sample → conversation locks → sales continues.

Do not attach the data sample to the first email. Do not send any campaign without Kiminou's explicit approval.

## Qualification gate
A dealership enters the send batch only when the record contains a verified business name, verified public website or dealer page, a working contact route, and at least two factual personalization points. If those are missing, mark `NEEDS_RESEARCH` instead of drafting fake personalization.

I was looking at [verified dealership detail #1] and [verified dealership detail #2]. We maintain a dealership data set that may be useful for your team depending on how you're handling prospecting and market coverage right now.

I can send you a small sample first so you can see exactly what is in it before we talk about anything else.

If you want the sample, just reply **yes**.

Kiminou
Artists and Athletes for Change

Absolutely. I attached the sample we mentioned so you can review the structure and usefulness before we go further.

If it lines up with what your team needs, reply with the market or coverage area you're most interested in and I'll make sure the next conversation is specific to that.

Kiminou
Artists and Athletes for Change

## Response states
`QUALIFIED` → verified and ready for approval
`NEEDS_RESEARCH` → fewer than two personalization facts or unverified contact
`APPROVED_TO_SEND` → Kiminou explicitly approved this row/batch
`SENT_1` → initial email sent
`YES_RECEIVED` → prospect explicitly requested sample
`SAMPLE_READY` → correct sample prepared
`SAMPLE_SENT` → sample delivered after YES
`SALES_CONVERSATION` → human sales conversation active
`FOLLOWUP_1`, `FOLLOWUP_2`, `FOLLOWUP_3`
`NO_RESPONSE`
`DO_NOT_CONTACT`
`CLOSED_WON`
`CLOSED_LOST`

## Definition of ready-to-launch
The campaign is ready when the real dealership list is loaded, duplicates/dead records are removed, every `QUALIFIED` row has two verified personalization facts, the sample contains no restricted/private data, and Kiminou has reviewed the exact first batch. This package does not authorize sending.
