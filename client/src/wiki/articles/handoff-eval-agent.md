Handoff Eval Agent asks a useful question: could somebody else take over this software without having to guess how it works?

## What it examines

The public project brief describes checks for tests, documentation, safety risks, and operational readiness. It also describes reports and suggested evaluation cases.

## Why a handoff needs evidence

A README alone cannot tell you whether an application is dependable. You need working instructions, tests that actually run, clear ownership, and a record of known issues.

## The limit of an automated score

A score can point somebody toward a problem. It cannot replace reviewing the code, testing the application, and making a decision about risk.
